Chorevo

Delete your account

You can delete your Chorevo account and its data at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Chorevo and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account, then Delete forever.

If the app asks you to sign in again first, sign out, sign back in, and repeat the steps.

Without the app

CONTACT DETAILS TO BE ADDED: the email address (or form) where users can request deletion without the app. Include the email address of the account in your request.

What is deleted

Your account, profile, username and profile photo; dances and videos you uploaded, with their likes and comments; your likes and comments on other dances; your practice history, points, badges, streaks and leaderboard entry; groups you own (with their posts and videos); your posts and replies in other groups; group invitations; and the push-notification tokens of your devices. Deletion happens straight away and can't be undone.
